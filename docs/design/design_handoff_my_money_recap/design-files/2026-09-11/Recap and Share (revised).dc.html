NEWD66, one demonstration artboard. At the "Large" accessibility size (about 1.35× the default ramp) the four screens become one vertical scroll; the PageDots at the top of each section are the section markers and tapping one scrolls to it. Nothing is clipped, nothing is capped: every card grows with its text. At default sizes nothing changes and the pager stays.
Type shown at 1.35×: headline l 64, m 54, compact 48, display 90, body 22, helper 19, buttons 22 in a 64pt pill. StatHero keeps its 80 → 56 → 40 rule on the formatted string. Illustrations are the one thing that shrinks, so the scroll stays about 3,400pt.
