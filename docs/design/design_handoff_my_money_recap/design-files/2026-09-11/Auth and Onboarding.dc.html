Row 1 · Launch, unlock and sign-in states
A0b · Unlock
My Money Recap
Face ID
D61 / D62: a session saved within the last 30 days exists, so the cover asks for Face ID (or Touch ID) and goes straight to Home. Works with no network — the unlock releases the Keychain entry and Home renders from cache. Each successful unlock extends the session another 30 days from last use.
The Face ID glyph and sheet are iOS-owned (LAContext); drawn for placement only, on the launch screen so nothing flashes between launch and Home. Three failures → A1 Sign in.

Row 4 · Flow
Flow · Launch to Home

{{ l.text }}
Launch checks for a saved session first (D61/D62): under 30 days old it unlocks with Face ID or the passcode and goes straight to Home, with no network round-trip; three failures fall back to Sign in. Onboarding progress is saved per screen; every step after the first has a back chevron and re-selects the saved answer on resume. Step 4 is blocked when the salary was skipped (D44): its prompt returns to step 3 and "Skip for now" goes to Home. Thumbnails are schematic; see rows 1–3 for the screens.
