C1 · Recap 1, cover
The Earned chip is gone — Salary is the only income in v1, so Money In and Earned would always be the same number. Two chips only: Saved £640 and Spent £1,760.
Money In = Salary landed inside the calendar month (£2,400 on the 25th). Saved = Money In − Spent = 2,400 − 1,760 = £640. Read-only, computed live on every open.
C2 · Recap 2, kept and streak
scale="max" matters here: Target is a benchmark, not a slice of a whole. Kept £640 fills the width; Target £480 sits at 75% of it. Kept% = Saved ÷ target amount = 640 ÷ 480 = 133%, clamped 0 to 999.
Streak = longest run of days with no spend, never crossing a month boundary: nothing is dated 12 to 20 September, so 9. Badge copy is "New badge unlocked." at 7 days or more, otherwise "Beat it next month."
Yellow ground: all text inverts to dark ink and the first chip is the ink inverse one. PageDots take onLight.
C3 · Recap 3, target and spending
Four bars is the cap; more overflow the artboard. The tail rolls into one line — "Gifts, Games, Toys, Other · 15%" — rather than being dropped. Bills 35, Rides 26, Snacks 14, Clothes 10 of £1,760.
The saving-target bar rides the piggy marker, never the skateboard. 133% clamps the bar at full; "£160 to spare" = Saved − target. Splurge = largest single spend, ties to the earliest date: £420, 27 September, 23.9% of Spent.
C4 · Recap 4, archetype
Archetype rules, in order: Saved ≥ target and Streak ≥ 7 → Squirrel. Saved ≥ target, Streak < 7 → Hustler. Saved < target and splurge ≥ 25% of Spent → Treat-Yourself. Otherwise → Slow Burner. September: 640 ≥ 480 and 9 ≥ 7.
No page dots: this is the shareable screen and screenshots must stay clean. CompareBars here use the default scale="total" — Saved and Spent are slices of Money In. The share button is the only tappable element on any recap screen.
