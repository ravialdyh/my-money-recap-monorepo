Launch → Sign in → email link or Apple/Google → four onboarding steps → first Home. Every screen sits on the violet recap ground; white cards carry the ink content. Elements built for this flow that don't yet exist in the system are tagged NEW in the notes. Yellow stickies are annotations, not UI.
