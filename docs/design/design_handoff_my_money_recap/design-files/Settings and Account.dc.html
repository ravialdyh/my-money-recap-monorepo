D2 · Profile
Profile
Your avatar Save
Display Name is 1 to 30 characters — letters, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes. Save is disabled until the name is valid and an avatar is chosen. Live: edit the name or tap an avatar.
Reuses A4's name field and AvatarGrid unchanged. Saving updates the Home greeting and every recap, past ones included — nothing about a recap is stored (PRD §7). Cards inside the white sheet keep the default white surface with a soft shadow so fields and tracks stay legible.
D5 · Currency
Currency
The full ISO 4217 list, sorted by name, searchable by code or name. The device locale's currency is preselected at setup. Reuses A5's searchable list; tapping a different row opens D6, never converts directly.
D6 · Currency confirm
Currency
All your amounts will be converted at today's rate, 1 GBP = 20,150 IDR. Your £2,400 salary becomes Rp 48.360.000. This can't be undone.
Convert Cancel
The rate is fetched before the sheet is shown. Saving Target percentages are never touched — only amounts convert: every salary row and every spend amount, once, in a single transaction, with the rate recorded.
IDR has no minor units, so converted amounts show no decimals — the contrast with GBP's two is the point. 2,400 × 20,150 = Rp 48.360.000; 1,760 × 20,150 = Rp 35.464.000, with id-ID separators.

D9 · Export spends
Export spends
Email me a CSV of every spend It goes to maya@example.com. Every spend you've ever logged, with its date, category, amount and note. Send it
One action, no options. The CSV is built server-side and emailed to the account address; nothing is written to the device. Amounts export in the current Currency's minor units.
D10 · Delete account
Delete account
This is permanent
Deleting removes your account, every spend you've logged, and every recap. We can't get any of it back.
 Delete my account
Step one of two. Nothing happens here except moving to D11. Coral is the only colour used for destructive controls; the card sits on the coral 100 tint to match.
D11 · Delete account, confirm

Two steps, and the second cannot be a tap. Live: the button enables only on an exact "DELETE". Signing out is immediate; the data and the identity record are gone within 24 hours.
