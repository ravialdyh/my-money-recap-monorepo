D3 · Salary and payday
Salary and payday
Payday
If the month is shorter, we use its last day. Save Changes apply to October and every month after. September's recap keeps the £2,400 it was built on.
Setting rows are append-only. Editing writes a new row effective from the current month; past months are never recomputed. Save is disabled until something changed and the salary is above zero.
Reuses A6's AmountField (fixed £ prefix, two minor units) and the 1-to-31 day picker, 25 as the ink pill, scrolled into view on open.

D5 · Currency
Currency
The full ISO 4217 list, sorted by name, searchable by code or name. The device locale's currency is preselected at setup. Reuses A5's searchable list; tapping a different row opens D6, never converts directly.
D6 · Currency confirm
Currency
All your amounts will be converted at today's rate, 1 GBP = 20,150 IDR. Your £2,400 salary becomes Rp 48,360,000. This can't be undone.
Convert Cancel
The rate is fetched before the sheet is shown. Saving Target percentages are never touched — only amounts convert: every salary row and every spend amount, once, in a single transaction, with the rate recorded.
IDR has no minor units, so converted amounts show no decimals — the contrast with GBP's two is the point. 2,400 × 20,150 = 48,360,000; 1,760 × 20,150 = 35,464,000. Cancel is Button variant="secondary", a real prop now.
